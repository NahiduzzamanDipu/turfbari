The team is ready, the boots are ready, and the mindset is ready. But where is the field? Finding a turf where a slot is available tonight, checking the rental price, and figuring out whom to call can take so much time that the evening is almost over before you know it. Meanwhile, turf owners spend their days managing notebooks, handling one phone call after another, and trying to keep track of outstanding payments.

To make things easier for both sides, **TurfBari** is here. A single platform where players can find turfs and send booking requests, while turf owners and managers can manage their entire turf business through an organized dashboard. **TurfBari – The Address of Every Turf.**

You don't need an account to search for turfs on TurfBari. Simply allow location access, and the platform will show you the nearest turfs first, along with their distances. You can also select a district and area or search by turf name. Each turf card displays photos, whether it's indoor or outdoor, the number of available fields, and the starting hourly rental price—all at a glance. No more calling ten different places just to find a field.

Once you visit your preferred turf's page, you'll find a photo gallery, available facilities, field information, opening hours, and rental prices for each time slot. With just one tap, you can get directions through the integrated map. Every turf also has its own QR code. Simply scan it to open that turf's booking page. Select your preferred date, field, and available time slot, enter your name and mobile number, and submit a booking request. Once the turf management confirms it, your game is officially on! You can also check your booking status anytime using your reference number.

Now, let's talk about turf owners. The dashboard gives you a complete overview of today's bookings, today's collections, monthly revenue, and total outstanding payments. The **Book a Slot** board lets you see which time slots are available, booked, or waiting for confirmation. If a team calls to make a booking, you can create it within seconds, including discounts and advance payments. Manage your fields, opening hours, peak-hour and weekend pricing, and customer booking history—all in one organized system, without depending on notebooks.

And what about managers? Owners can add managers directly from the Team page, giving each manager their own separate login. Managers can handle daily bookings, confirm booking requests, record payments, and manage customers. However, changing rental prices, managing expenses, viewing reports, and controlling the team remain exclusively under the owner's authority. Responsibilities are shared, but control stays in your hands.

For every booking, you can see the total rental amount, the amount already paid, and the remaining balance. Whether payments come through cash, bKash, or Nagad, every transaction is recorded alongside the booking. The reports section gives you a clear picture of collections, expenses, and net income, along with daily collection charts and booking statistics for each field. Need a report? Download it in Excel with just one click.

For players, TurfBari makes finding a turf and sending booking requests simple and hassle-free. For owners, it means organized business operations and transparent financial records. For managers, it means clearly defined responsibilities. When the hassles outside the field are reduced, the joy of playing on it becomes even greater.
